WHERE THE NAME COMES FROM

Hiri is a Buddhist concept — the inner restraint that stops unethical action, self-enforced, independent of observation. We didn't choose it for how it sounds. Procurement, more than most functions, runs on exactly that kind of unwatched integrity.

Every quote we source, every vendor we onboard, every contract we negotiate on a client's behalf passes through the same test: would we stand behind this if nobody ever reviewed the file? Hiriwork is that principle turned into a working method.
